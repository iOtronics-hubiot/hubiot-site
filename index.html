Why IOtronics

Computing has outgrown its original interfaces.

Modern devices conceal increasingly sophisticated memory structures, embedded logic and software-defined behaviour. Conventional acquisition and analysis methods were not designed for this scale of diversity, volatility or operational sensitivity.

Move beyond extraction.

IOtronics is designed to interact with digital information at a deeper architectural layer—preserving fidelity, reducing intervention and producing richer representations for analysis.

01Architecture before abstraction

Prepare information for machine reasoning.

The platform is intended to convert low-level acquired data into structured computational representations that support digital twins, investigative workflows and future AI-assisted analysis.

02From signal to representation
The platform

One architecture. Multiple technology layers.

IOtronics extends the original Mantle Labs concept into a broader platform spanning acquisition hardware, computational modelling, intelligent analysis and future purpose-built silicon.

01 / ACQUISITION

High-fidelity acquisition architecture

Hardware-assisted methods intended to acquire information from modern memory architectures while minimising disruption to the source environment.

02 / RECONFIGURABLE HARDWARE

Adaptable embedded logic

Programmable architectures provide a pathway for supporting diverse devices, interfaces and evolving memory technologies.

03 / COMPUTATIONAL REPRESENTATION

Digital twin generation

Transform acquired information into structured representations suitable for reconstruction, simulation and advanced investigative analysis.

04 / COGNITIVE ANALYSIS

AI-assisted investigation

Support analysts by correlating acquired artefacts, reconstructing activity and surfacing meaningful relationships across large evidence sets.

05 / FUTURE SILICON

Purpose-built ASIC architecture

A long-term pathway toward specialised silicon that brings acquisition, transformation and analysis closer to the physical source of digital information.
