Workflow

Five connected stages.

Each stage preserves continuity between the physical source, the reconstructed evidence and the investigator's conclusions.

01

Acquire

Capture complex physical storage through a controlled, read-only pathway.

02

Reconstruct

Interpret translation layers, memory organisation and filesystems.

03

Twin

Create a high-fidelity virtual evidence environment.

04

Analyse

Discover artefacts, relationships, behaviours and investigative patterns.

05

Explain

Produce contextual findings while retaining investigator control.

Digital twin

Preserve once. Investigate repeatedly.

Revisit and collaborate around a virtual evidence environment without repeatedly interacting with the original device.

Investigative AI

Accelerate discovery without surrendering judgement.

Prioritise, correlate and explain findings while keeping investigative authority with the human practitioner.
